Model assumptions and metric definitions

The desk accepts tickets from 08:00 to 20:00. Early shifts run 08:00–16:00 and late shifts 12:00–20:00. Every agent handles one ticket at a time. Service continues to completion if a shift ends mid-ticket. No new service starts at or after 20:00.

The seed fixes each ticket’s arrival minute, service duration and urgency. Urgent-first changes the order of waiting work, without interrupting service already in progress. Tickets of equal priority retain arrival order. The simulation excludes abandonment, breaks and differences in agent skill.

Wait is the time from arrival to first service. Median and 90th-percentile waits use the nearest-rank method for tickets started by the replay time. Tickets still waiting are reported separately. Comparing percentiles alongside the started counts helps explain why serving more tickets can raise the observed wait. Shift overrun is the sum of minutes agents spend finishing work past their scheduled shift end.
