How to read the results

Every Monday, the model estimates the next seven days using earlier parcel counts, calendar signals and scheduled promotions. The benchmark repeats the volume from the same weekday one week earlier.

Coverage measures the share of observations inside the forecast band. The band uses each depot’s errors from a separate 12-week calibration period. Its target is 90% and actual coverage varies.
